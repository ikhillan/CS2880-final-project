$P(D)$ its DA placement, and $S(D)=C(D)-P(D)$. Then

\Delta P=\Delta C-\Delta S.

\begin{frame}{Broader considerations and sources}
\small
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Access.} Individual retention can preserve existing exclusion.
Report outcomes for applicants without an initial offer alongside incumbent safety.
\item \textbf{Behavior.} Families may ignore advice, add options, or change ranks.
Fixed-report replays do not identify deployment effects.
\item \textbf{Data and participation.} Keep pupil-level records private and respect
source restrictions. Any field intervention needs appropriate institutional review
and a plan for participant information, correction and complaints.
\item \textbf{Resources.} Recommendations redistribute access to existing capacity.
They do not create affordable apartments, job vacancies, or school seats.
\end{itemize}
\source{\href{https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026/CallForPapers}{ICML 2026 call: impact statement};
\href{https://neurips.cc/public/EthicsGuidelines}{NeurIPS ethics guidelines};
\href{https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/MainTrackHandbook}{NeurIPS 2026 handbook: checklist}.
These motivate consideration of harms; our proposed safeguards have not been tested.}
\end{frame}
