# Chilean school recommendations in the field

Chile’s 2021 School Explorer randomized families to different ways of finding schools. Comparing highlights plus counts with a basic map, the share ranking a qualifying school second increased by 5.4 percentage points. The assignment estimate was +0.2 points, with an approximate 95% interval from −2.0 to +2.4. The published study measures school attributes and has different analytic samples for the outcomes. A separate 2010 experiment directly measured enrollment at schools listed on a report card. [2021 trial and outcomes](trials/FIELD_TABLES.md) · [2010 report cards](trials/REPORT_CARD.md) · [Public-data audit](trials/DATA_ACCESS.md)

Our national 2021 comparison uses the same application year and three entry grades as the Explorer. The two 2019 predictors place 183,634 and 183,325 of the 200,591 pupils linked to the April register. Rank-informed coverage of actual enrollment is higher by 703 pupils. We matched the published initial offer or nonassignment for every one of the 461,223 applicants before the menu tests. The experimental assignment and actual recommendations shown to a given family are unavailable in the public files we located. [Source archives, reproduction instructions and counts](cohort/README.md)

The mixed adoption experiment changes menus for randomly selected pupils and re-clears the entire 2021 national cohort. At 50% uptake the average own-menu placement effect is −0.0321 percentage points. Under complete rollout the placement rate changes by −0.1540 points. We preserve every paired simulation in aggregate form. Exact small-market enumeration checks the relation between these estimands and the possibility of opposite signs.
