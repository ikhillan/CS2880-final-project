# Later-cohort check and shared benchmarks

This local record precedes our acquisition or inspection of the 2024 and 2025 regular application cohorts for this analysis. The 2022–23 comparisons and the development rental pilot are known. This is a timestamped internal plan, not an external registration.

1. Include both publicly listed later Chilean regular application cohorts (application years 2024 and 2025), all grades and all applicants under the existing eligibility and continuity rules. Apply the frozen 2019 rank-blind and rank-informed coefficients and historical program counts with the archived tie seeds and five discretionary options plus the continuity entitlement. Do not refit. Primary criterion in *each* additional cohort: relative to rank-blind, rank-informed increases the number of initial historical offers visible AND decreases the number of applicants placed by pooled DA. Report input changes, exact counts, rates, full-list replay agreement and exceptions, including a failure of either sign. No inference based on treating pupils as independent. Where a required priority measure is missing or has changed meaning, document the difference. Keep incompatible years out of the fixed-rule comparison instead of silently imputing.
2. Apply exactly the same reference policy in the original four panels. Retain the first five feasible choices in applicant order, against uniformly sampled five feasible options with the same entitlements. The renter ordering is latent in the simulation, the school ordering is reported. Use 48 seeded uniform menus, the deterministic first five, and the full-list allocation for reference. Report changes in coverage, actual placements in replay and, where calculated, reference-partner assignment, with all applicants in the denominator. Report every panel regardless of sign.
3. For site-specific policy pairs, use independent Bernoulli adoption at p=0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 1. Use paired complementary uniforms and report Monte Carlo SE separately from market variability. Keep realized rankings, capacities and candidate sets fixed within a policy comparison. Quantify displacement among applicants whose two possible menus are identical. Describe NYC preference/priority perturbations and the school quota sweep as exploratory, distinct from the later-cohort criterion.
