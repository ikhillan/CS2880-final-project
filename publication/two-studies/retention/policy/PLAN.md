# Pre-assignment design and its held-out lottery test

Frozen on 2026-11-14 before opening the new lottery batch or scoring any new policy against 2022–24 enrollment. Primary years are the 2022, 2023, 2024 applications. The two original 2019 offer predictors and published menus A (blind), B (rank-informed) are fixed. The decision precedes the lottery but follows submission of the complete reports. The proposed menu keeps A∩B, uses only A∪B and retains A's per-pupil quota. It neither uses the realized lottery nor receives current-cohort initial offers or subsequent April labels. Any evaluation grant of those labels is logically and programmatically separate. Numerical score design uses only 2021 training pupils differing across A/B and their April 2022 enrollment (available before the 2022 application round); predictions use the frozen 2019 scores, original report positions and menu structure. The later allocation cohorts and previously released aggregate comparisons are known. The new held-out lottery draws have not been run or examined for this policy.

**Finite-support receipt guarantee.** Simulate the A baseline for all 1,024 original matched-course priority draws (batch 513). At grades above pre-K permit only substitutions that leave unchanged the proposal prefix through the final hold of every pupil in every design lottery. In pre-K require every assigned baseline edge; permit a B-only edge if it never blocks the old detailed allocation in any design draw OR if the course's sum of four ordinary seat capacities covers every distinct applicant ever listing that course in A∪B. The latter admits a move regardless of who would claim the course elsewhere. Every pupil can apply to all seat types subject to priority, with a further continuity fallback where applicable. For every year and draw re-clear A and selected policy audit all pupils' gross exits. Any observed design exit is a failed theorem check, never a rationale for tuning the held-out sample.

**Objective.** Bin 2021 applicant-school pairs from A∪B for linked switchers by best submitted course rank (1,2,3,4,5,6–8,9+), maximum blind offer probability (cuts .1,.25,.5,.75), and maximum informed offer probability (same cuts). Count the April school's observed membership at each bin. Shrink a cell with 40 pseudo-observations to the estimate for its rank group, whose rate has itself 40 pseudo-observations at the global rate. No course, pupil, school or district ID is a feature. Freeze fitted counts before scoring later cohorts. Maximize the sum of these predicted per-school hits for each switcher's selected distinct schools over the finite eligibility constraints. Required schools earn no further hit for another course. Select the best-scored new school by its earliest course until slots are filled; when score ties favor an old school then earlier course; fill redundant courses by preferring old and then earlier. Then among equivalent score solutions prefer A over modifying any applicant row. This is a historical prediction objective evaluated out of time. No use of 2022–24 labels for tuning or model choice.

**Test.** Fresh matched independent-course lotteries (same design generator; new batch 8615729, draw numbers 1–128 independently in each year); fresh family-by-school lotteries (batch 8615731, 1–32 in each year) as sensitivity. Audit unrestricted B on all primary test draws. Compare protected screens with their A baseline including applicants with identical menus. Record gross incumbent exits, gains, moves between schools, course/seat changes, and number of draw-years with an exit. The primary independent quantity is each year's probability that at least one old recipient loses a seat. Use one-sided 95 percent exact binomial upper limits by year within each lottery design and mark the absence of a probability guarantee outside the training support. Register hits are evaluated once per fixed menu after the design rules, model and all three masks are frozen. Report signed, gross and subgroup results, including any null or negative changes. The statistical unit for lottery risk is a national allocation, not a pupil.
