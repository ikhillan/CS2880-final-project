# Lottery-independent seat-retention rule

Design before opening the rule's test lotteries. The source uses only submitted choices and linked-family structure, the blind/informed menu candidates, and public vacancy counts. No actual offers, realized priorities, tie-break realizations, or April registrations enter construction of the prospective menu.

At each grade, from the highest downward, call a course ample when its total typed seats are at least the number of distinct applicants who could list it under the union of the two original recommendations. At an ample course no pupil can exhaust all seat categories without an assignment. In these Chilean rules all applicants can propose to all four physical types. A protected current-school fallback also guarantees a seat. For family links relevant on a pupil's union row, require that all of its older relatives' final course assignments are invariant across menu policies. Fallback and ample courses certify retention for all pupils with a relevant common stop. On other rows allow only swaps strictly after the first common guaranteed stop in the operational order for every possible promotion, or swaps on courses with no physical seats and no current fallback. Rows unchanged are also possible. For pupils who could lose a seat, and older pupils whose destination can affect a younger relative, use a stronger component safeguard described in the implementation before any test lottery is opened.

Primary label-free objective is the sum of submitted-rank scores (prefer earlier listed courses) on each enabled row, with common courses and the old quota fixed. Report menu changes, realized April outcome scored offline, gross loss/entry, school/contract switches, and total headcount on fresh simulated Chilean lotteries. A separate retrospective optimization of historical April hits in exactly this structural menu family supplies an attainable lower bound for the general seat-retention problem. No extrapolation from a held-out zero to a universal promise is necessary for a rule proved safe.

Freeze those reached pupils' entire blind rows. Treat ample courses as absorbing for course destinations: a proposer eventually gets that course or a more preferred one, independent of competitors, since all union demand fits; which seat type they obtain may change. Equivalently remove every course after the first ample/current guaranteed stop from each feasible report, with family promotion handled conservatively by using every possibly earlier edge.
