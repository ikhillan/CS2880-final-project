# Sensitivity after the 16-scenario result
The initial 16-draw experiment showed universal failure of the market-wide holdout certificate and far fewer incumbent exits than switching every slot. Two exploratory checks address the obvious alternative explanation and sensitivity to the number of draws.

1. Use nested training supports of 16, 64, 256 and 1,024 draws under batch 513. At each checkpoint compute the zero-exception optimum. Reuse the original 64 evaluation draws and the recorded priorities. This is a sensitivity analysis on a known evaluation design.
2. At each checkpoint create eight comparison menus. Group rows by the count of slots on which the original A and B menus disagree. Within each group permute the number of swaps the certified policy made per row. Choose that many old and new slots uniformly without replacement in each row. Retain entitlements and common courses. This fixes the total number of edits, number of changed rows and the distribution of edits by row size. Evaluate all eight on the recorded order; also evaluate the seed-0 comparison menu on all 64 simulated holdouts. At 16 draws, cross-check the original budget-zero menu by exact equality of all recorded outcome counts.
