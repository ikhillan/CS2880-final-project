# Design fixed before computing the new policy's outcomes

Earlier work in the project used five courses with the largest frequencies of being a pupil's partner across 64 independent full-report lottery clears. We know all earlier endpoint results, including sparse frequency ties. The new follow-up uses the same 64 draws without observing the evaluation lottery, offers or enrollment. The eligible graph, submitted orders, coarse priorities, capacities, and protected continuity options are fixed. It is a prospective calculation following submission of all district reports.

After each simulated full-list DA, a course is individually attainable at that draw's cutoffs if it has a vacancy or would retain the pupil at the priority of its lowest held pupil (including equality for its own holder). In each pupil's fixed-quota menu, maximize lexicographically (1) number of draws with at least one individually attainable listed course, (2) number of simulated full-list partner hits, (3) total numbers of attainable listed courses summed over draws as a backup tie break, (4) earlier submitted ranks in lexicographic order. Protect continuity as specified. Objective (1) is a distinct-scenario coverage objective. Objective (3) chooses additional attainable alternatives even when one already exists. Equal priority at cutoff is allowed only for the pupil already holding it. Actual clearing and the priorities of other pupils can change when lists change. Fixed-cutoff attainability is a surrogate, not a guarantee.

Primary comparator is the existing partner-frequency policy with its original submitted-rank tie break. A diagnostic ablation maximizes only (2), (3), (4), i.e. changes the baseline's ties. Use the original eight batches and all three random-tie cohorts 2022–24; the 2025 within-course tie rule changed and is outside this design. Evaluate at 64 draws. Report full sample placement, offer visibility, family first choice and full-list partner visibility for every cohort and batch. Primary descriptive comparisons are paired within batch/year. Include register-linked enrollment as a historical scoring endpoint if the existing linkage files are accessible. No 2025 performance claim, inference across kernel draws as independent cohorts, or deployment claim. Include all adverse signs. Keep aggregate outputs only.

## Post hoc sensitivity following the original 24 evaluations

Keep every cohort, original batch number, draw count, menu objective, and recorded evaluation priority. In each simulated full-DA draw replace independent course-specific within-tier rankings with one uniformly random ordering of pupils, used by every course within each of its own public priority tiers. Refit both coverage and partner-frequency policies under the same alternative kernel. This deliberately changes the simulator's correlation assumption; it is a sensitivity on the original recorded-priority replays, not an estimate for an actual common-lottery regime.
