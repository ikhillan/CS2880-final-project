November 14, 2026 UTC. After the original simulation was launched, field evidence about the position of added schools motivated this additional diagnostic. For every original uniform and Haiku menu in the 480 markets, add the same highest-ranked hidden feasible option as in the ranked-recovery scenario. Place it last after all originally displayed options. Compare the paired policy results to the scenario in which renters propose in original reported order. Compute first-choice assignments on underlying original model preferences in all cases. Verify that every originally occupied apartment remains occupied within each arm and that any unemployed-to-placed entrant from a restricted market receives their added edge. Count incumbent exits and entrants per arm; changes in who holds a unit can occur even under suffix extension. Run independent simultaneous DA with the altered applicant orders, compare both arms and run a direct continuation from the stored DA tentative assignment and exhausted-proposal state. No estimate of actual renter behavior.

Before a second new run, fix a second tail policy that makes every omitted feasible listing available at the bottom in original order. Compute placement and first choices for policy A versus B, plus gross changes and stability at the original preference profile measured by counts of renters in at least one blocking pair. Full options are identical for every applicant by construction, even when the sequence of applications differs. Report unchanged application order separately from unchanged option set.
