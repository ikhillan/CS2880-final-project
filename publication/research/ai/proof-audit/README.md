# Protecting provisional placements during a recommendation rollout

A posted offer can reveal enough information to protect an applicant's continued placement even if the platform cannot observe landlord priorities. The theorem and the quota-constrained recommendation rule are in [theory.tex](theory.tex). The model requires stable, unit-capacity assignments, strict rankings, fixed inventory, fixed lists and preferences for the incumbent group, and consistent priority orderings within that group across rounds. Rent, location, units, ranks and higher-income applicants' placement status can all change. Moving simulated offers into this two-round interpretation requires a baseline assignment already observed or computed by the platform.

- [guardrail.py](guardrail.py) constructs threat graphs, computes their maximum matching, chooses the best additive-score display at a specified matching bound, or freezes the fewest original rows within the zero-threat rule. Positive-budget optimization is NP-hard even at quota one. The zero-budget closure is polynomial.
- [verify_guardrail.py](verify_guardrail.py) checks every fixed-list ordering and receiver priority ordering for two applicants/three units and three applicants/two units, with all possible stable outcomes on both sides of a switch. Original outsider reports yielding the same original match are collapsed. The new outsider reports are allowed to reorder preferences; this also tests the weaker assumption used by the proof. The other checks compare integer programming against complete enumeration, realize every 3-by-3 graph at the bound, and check small vertex-cover reductions. For each simulated closure check the code enumerates *all* superset freeze sets and all stable outcomes after repair. Counts are in [checks.json](checks.json).
- [evaluate_guardrail.py](evaluate_guardrail.py) uses the learned experiment's output arrays and inference implementation. It preserves each row's original number of recommendations and tests stability on every realized display. Each split/score file reports raw count totals and paired confidence intervals. [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md) specifies the comparison.

## A voluntary ceiling on a replacement unit's asking rent

The post-hoc [rent veto protocol](RENT-VETO-PROTOCOL.md) records why the team tested universal simulated take-up. Applicants who already held an offer can exclude more expensive displayed units from their proposals. Their original offer survives these deletions, and the original stable matching remains stable on the restricted display. Applying the rank theorem to that starting market protects rank and asking rent for any subset who consent. The platform keeps the quota in the visible recommendations. Every higher-income applicant's eventual displayed and effective lists are identical to the comparison guardrail.

[exact_rents.py](exact_rents.py) rebuilds the sampled listings from their original catalogue and seed keys and verifies float32 feature alignment on all 3,913,500 feasible edges. The veto replay uses them if they have not been rebuilt locally. [rent_veto.py](rent_veto.py) uses the source double-precision asking rents for dollar comparisons and records outcomes, actual participant transitions, and paired simulation intervals.
