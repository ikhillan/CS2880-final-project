# How provisional offer holders fare after the switch

This follow-up was specified after checking placement counts for the contextual score and its priority-blind guardrail, before inspecting any rank or rent-burden changes. Use all paired summer/winter, ordinary/enriched test markets with the frozen higher-only contextual top-five policies. For each of (original uniform, direct contextual) and (original uniform, priority-blind guarded contextual), restrict to lower-income applicants assigned a unit by the original uniform display. Both policies retain the underlying preferences and lower-income lists.

Count strictly better, equal and strictly worse simulated renter rank, with an unmatched outcome worse than any eligible listing. Count any change of partner including losing a partner; distinguish a transfer between two units and a transition to unmatched. Because preferences are strict, weak equality means the same unit. Report ordinal shares of all original matched lower-income renters. For original recipients matched under each given new policy, calculate the paired mean change in annualized asking-rent-to-income burden (new minus original), and the shares with higher or lower burden. The original list and new policy can select different sets of stayers; present a second calculation restricting both policies to people matched in all three assignments. Report gross rank losses among applicants under a placement guarantee, including those losing a match under the direct rule. Treat the latent rank as a simulator quantity; use rent/cap × 0.40 since the generator sets cap to 40 percent of monthly income. For rent equality compare the archived float32 rent/cap values as stored. No parameter changes or new model selection.

Report raw counts and within-cell rates standardized with the same borough weights and equal month weights as the main evaluation, with paired 5,000 bootstrap resamples of simulation seed blocks within each panel. In any replicate with empty conditional denominators in a cell, omit that replicate *for that conditional estimand only* and record how many were omitted. If the original full-panel denominator is zero in a cell, give only raw pooled counts and explicitly flag the unavailable standardized metric for that panel.
