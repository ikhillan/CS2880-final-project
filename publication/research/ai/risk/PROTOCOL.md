# Market-level rollout calibration

Fixed before the paired assignment outputs for these exposures were available. For each of ordinary and enriched summer markets the candidate family is transparent, pointwise, and contextual scores, each returned with row top five or with the same assignment anchor. Only higher-income rows change. The lower-income outcome is the event that at least one renter matched under all-uniform becomes unmatched. A gain for someone else never cancels this event. Each block contains four boroughs crossed with three months. In each design take the weighted fraction of the block's 12 markets with the event using fixed recent-mover borough weights renormalized and equal month weights. Calibrate all 12 upper bounds together with a one-sided bounded-mean KL-Chernoff inversion and Bonferroni at 5%. Dependence inside a block and between candidates is allowed. The 32 summer calibration blocks beginning at 2026120900 do not participate in neural training, checkpoint selection or reported test results. Fail rather than drop cells if any are missing.

For each score and decoder separately consider flipping one fair-source independent coin of deployment probability q per entire market; adopt for every higher-income row on heads, otherwise keep all-uniform. For maximum tolerated risk t in {0.01,0.05,0.10}, choose q=min(1,t/U) using the simultaneous bound. Publish all twelve policies and expected held-out risks, computed by integrating the coin, on both summer and winter. Only summer has a same-generator guarantee. Publish the unrestricted risk, the calibrated uptake and the expected lower-income net change separately. Market randomization by itself can protect a fraction of markets through nondeployment while allowing losses in others. It cannot deliver person-level guarantees. No cross-market interference is assumed.
