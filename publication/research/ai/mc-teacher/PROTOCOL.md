# Conditional simulation comparator

Fixed before scoring. Take all 24 cells in the first eight summer test blocks (2026120500–507) and first eight winter transfer blocks (2026120700–707). At inference use only feasibility and the generator's observable utilities. Draw 128 independent new latent markets per observed market, keeping costs, distances and screening fixed. Applicant utility adds independent N(0,0.25) listing and N(0,0.25) pair shocks (variance notation). Listing utility adds independent standard normal pair shocks. Compute exact full-exposure DA for each draw and count the assigned partners, including the outside option. Mask nonedges. Compare 16, 64 and 128 draws using their shared stochastic prefix; break discrete top-probability ties with the transparent score and then a fixed seeded permutation. The held-out allocation is never used in this conditional simulation. Compare the probability ranking with the frozen learned and transparent policies on the exact same cells. Primary statistic is full-market partner recovery; also report total matches and losses relative to full DA under all-row top five. A generator-informed comparator has information about the shock law unavailable in a housing platform. It is an approximation to the best rule for coverage under this simulator. Neither Bayes coverage nor DA is a maximum-placement objective.
