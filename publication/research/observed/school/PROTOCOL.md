# Chile SAE observed-preference stress test

Design frozen after reading the 2019 release's schemas and official method descriptions, before computing either prediction success or menu-policy outcomes. Use the regular round of application-year 2019 for training and 2020 for temporal testing. These cover all 14 grade codes (pre-kindergarten through the last secondary grade) in nationwide administrative files. After the main comparison, replay the frozen 2019 model on the next consecutively available nationwide outcome, 2021, if its schema and access permit. We will report both test years regardless of signs.

Use the reported course within school as an alternative. Accept the submitted order as the applicant preference order over the reported menu. Protect a recorded current-school entitlement as a free fallback outside the k discretionary slots; never score it, truncate at this entitlement if present, and distinguish securing an actual assignment from receiving a discretionary course. Countrywide standard DA is run separately by grade using actual published vacancies, returning/sibling/staff/alumnus priority tiers and actual published course-level lottery order, breaking unreported ties deterministically. This fixed-priority, pooled-seat rule abstracts from special seat reserves, dynamic sibling priorities, block applications and official sequencing. Report all official-versus-full-replay assignment agreement rates including the outside option. Observed assignments are initial offers before responses, not enrollments. Use the recorded official partner as a separate coverage benchmark alongside the full-list modeled DA partner. Report the full sample, with separate results for applicants whose lists actually exceed quota.

Learn a pooled edge probability rule using only the 2019 files, fitting prior-year official initial offers conditional on (a) announced seats, (b) school-program application load among all submitted options, (c) known priority tier, and (d) prior-year destination prevalence via smoothed prior-year program offer rate. Exclude applicant identifiers, individual lottery numbers, focal rank, official outcomes and geography from the primary scoring features at deployment; the set of submitted choices and aggregate demand are assumed known at pruning time. Use the 2019 no-leak within-training prior rate as leave-one-out to fit the 2019 coefficients. Use fixed basis ridge logistic regression, with no search over rankers. Ties use a seeded random real. Compare at most five discretionary courses with uniformly selected courses, under identical applicants and post-shortlist DA. Secondary quotas 1,2,3. Score all applicants including singleton lists and no-options cases. Run 48 independent uniform menu replications, comparing average paired effects at fixed realized priorities. Additional benchmarks: first k by submitted preference, and similarly trained rank-aware score that sees declared preference position. Uniform menus retain true order among included options. Fifty-eight independent markets are not implied by two years or many random policies. Give Monte Carlo variability explicitly conditional on the observed market and report year-specific complete-count results. No imputed renter-like edges, synthetic priority shocks, or reserve-eligibility inferences. Raw application data, obscured applicant IDs or individual lotteries are not exported.

Primary definition: relative to uniform, the learned rank-blind policy has the prediction-versus-placement reversal only if the probability of including the full-report DA partner among applicants matched on the full report rises and the fraction of all applicants assigned by DA falls. Official destination coverage is a complementary check using the fully observed D1 label. Recovery counts include protected fallback when the reference destination is the fallback. The supplementary offer-in-declared-list placement rate excludes an administratively appended fallback. Formal evidence is a finite-replay policy comparison.

Implementation details fixed before scoring: beta-binomial smoothing with prior strength 20 and the national average positive discretionary-edge offer rate; a seven-coefficient main-effects model plus squared log pressure, quadratic/linear historical log-odds and grade/degree terms, learning rank-position terms only in the named extension. Report the actual fitted feature names with coefficients. Use grade as the complete market unit for sign-count summaries, without treating national grade groups as a random sample. Audit retention of submitted non-appended assignments and split results by the administrative disadvantaged-student indicator, if recorded. Count official numerical sentinel/blank destinations as no offer; report official-only destinations outside benchmark lists. Local record linkage uses only masked identifiers, never transmitted to a compute cluster. 

Follow-up after inspecting the two holdouts: verify the full and five-choice deterministic endpoints with an independently written simultaneous-offers DA routine using the same realized inputs. Test whether the secondary rank-information contrast persists within each fixed public subsample (disadvantage designation, unaffected short lists and longer lists). Remove reliance on Monte Carlo for inclusion under the uniform baseline by summing the exact per-edge inclusion probabilities. As a mechanism check, repeat the five-choice comparison on pooled regular seats only and, separately, on the published total vacancy count with zero capacity uplift (returners still get first priority, meaning their fallback is guaranteed displayed but no longer always guaranteed assigned). These are sensitivity benchmarks and not reconstructions of the actual SAE reserve and family rules.
