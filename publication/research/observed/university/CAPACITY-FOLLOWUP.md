# Measuring display capacity

Secondary diagnostic. On the 2026 regular-seat market with overbooking at k=5, compute the exact maximum number of disjoint assignments respecting program capacities, for each deterministic policy and for the first 16 of the 64 preregistered uniform seeds. Use identical offer lists, seat counts and priorities as the primary run. On the archived two NYC panels, compute the analogous capacity in all 2,304 observed-feature simulation markets for uniform and contextual row-top-five. Report the decomposition of the placement contrast as the change in visible maximum cardinality minus the change in the shortfall of DA relative to that maximum. This is a mathematical accounting identity. The latter component allows unstable assignments in its benchmark and is not a count of avoidable DA errors. The national uniform mean in this diagnostic is conditional on 16 draws. Do not select arms based on sign.
