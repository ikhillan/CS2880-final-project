# Pre-analysis plan for regular Chilean university admissions

Frozen after reading the ministry dictionaries, field names and aggregate status/capacity counts, before solving any PAES market, fitting a predictor, or computing shortlist outcomes. The question is whether better recovery and worse placement coexist in the temporally held-out 2026 national market. 2024 supplies prior program baselines; 2025 supplies supervised training labels; 2026 supplies evaluation. Applicants may reapply; no applicant identifiers or applicant histories enter features. Each year is a single interdependent market. Monte Carlo standard errors reflect shortlist randomness conditional on one entire cohort. Do not make population claims using applicants as independent treatment replicates.

* Use scored regular-track applications certified admissible or passed over by the released administrative status codes 24/25/26, jointly, never distinguishing them as a test feature. Every applicant with a regular application (including no retained edge) is in the placement denominator. Raw identifiers are private join keys only. Do not package individual public records. Assumed school priorities: higher program-specific reported weighted score, ties by a fixed seeded lottery. Applicant ranks follow submitted preference order. Each program has its two published regular seat counts plus both announced overbooking counts. Exclude unknown programs, invalid scores and duplicate edges, report all exclusions. Reserved channels and later waitlist/enrollment are outside this model. Primary oracle is full admissible regular-list DA. Independently compare this oracle to the recorded regular offer (code 24). Evaluate coverage among observed recipients separately.
* Train one fixed ridge logistic edge classifier to predict the prior year's full-list DA partner, using current reported rank plus size of own list, weighted selection score, interactions with preceding year's program admission cutoff and congestion. No test-label feature, program-specific current cutoff, competitor's test outcome, applicant ID or sex. Missing historical programs get a disclosed fallback. Fit on all 2025 admissible edges, using 2024 program signals. Apply frozen parameters to 2026 edges using 2025 signals. Candidate set and rank inputs are already reported; this is post-report curation. Comparing against uniform diagnoses aligned/disaligned policy effects under this design; applicant top-ranked k and a historical-cutoff rule are practical benchmarks.
* For k=5 (primary), 1, 3 and 10 (sensitivity), keep min(k, degree) submitted alternatives. Re-sort kept edges by the original applicant ranking for DA. Pair every policy within the exact same cohort, capacity vector and score-based priorities. Use 64 seeded uniform market draws per quota, common keys across quotas; uniform partner coverage also has an exact analytic expectation. Compare program counts, recovered full-list and recorded partners, fraction placed, originally matched who lose, new placements, unavoidable no-options, nonbinding lists. Check capacity, stability and proposal bounds; serial and independent batch DA must return equal full-list assignments. In signed contrasts use percentage points and label conditional randomness explicitly.
* Repeat k=5 under base capacity excluding overbooking; report fidelity to recorded regular offers and outcomes of fixed scorers. No fitted alternative or scenario selected for the headline. The administrative baseline is a diagnostic of external relevance, not evidence of actual intervention effects. Release reproducible code, checksums, aggregate outcomes, and acquisition links only.
