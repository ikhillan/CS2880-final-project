# Secondary test with recorded offer labels and outcome-blind submitted lists

Frozen after seeing the primary 2026 logistic comparisons. The primary logistic model improves both metrics against uniform; its advantage in partner recovery over a prior-cutoff heuristic costs placements. Test these conclusions on a common cohort using supervised labels from actual 2025 regular offers. Strip all administrative status information from 2026 model inputs and from the construction of its recommendation candidate rows: keep every regular application with a valid program, submitted rank and strictly positive weighted selection score. Collapse duplicate applicant–program rows to the earliest rank. Record missing-score, absent-program and duplicate counts. Keep applicants with zero rows. This is a robustness definition of eligibility; a positive score need not meet every official entry rule. Use 2025 status 24 only to supply the training target (outside if no eligible recorded offer), and preceding-year program cutoff summaries derived from historical offers. No test-year status except for reading official labels in evaluation. Do not transmit direct or pseudonymous applicant identifiers to compute nodes.

Fit two equal-size permutation-equivariant row models to the observed 2025 offer, conditional on the applicant's reported candidate set. Five numeric features: weighted score, score minus past cutoff, log prior demand/seat, log current advertised capacity, missing-history indicator. A sixth is log submitted rank for the informed model, zero for the blind model. A shared edge MLP and mean-pooling across own valid options plus an outside class give categorical probabilities. Two hidden layers of width 32 + shared context, 12 epochs, AdamW 0.002, batch 512, three initialization seeds (8431,8432,8433). Use the final epoch, average probabilities. The blind model sees which programs an applicant listed but not their ordering. Evaluate frozen models and uniform, own-rank and historical-cutoff policies using the same positive-score support; quotas 1,3,5,10 and 32 uniform draws for placement. Report overlap between modeled and observed offers and missing support. This analysis responds to an outcome-admissibility and training-label concern. It reuses the 2026 test cohort, and is not a new held-out replication.
