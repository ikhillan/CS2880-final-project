# Outcome-blind ranking check after the first comparison

After the first, noisy event holdout comparison, check whether the particular ordering created by the learned scores is predictive relative to randomly assigning those scores. Keep test responses, trained coefficients, group structure, full partner distributions, each original row's quota and sparse graph fixed. Randomize the edge order independently within every proposing row. Under this independent random-order comparison, random top five equals uniform top five without replacement. Use 4,096 independent draws of the unknown rankings within tied liking scores; in each draw compare the frozen learned score to a new randomized top five, keeping the one-capacity assignment rule unchanged. Report conditional randomization uncertainty as Monte Carlo standard error for the paired mean, and preserve the separate six-event-day uncertainty about external generalization. Do not treat repeated simulations as new empirical markets. A low conditional MC error cannot make a day-level interval narrower. Women proposing, consent withheld, quota five, same tests. Also split coverage loss by the fraction of receivers who receive any exposure and count observed mutual yeses on shown edges.
