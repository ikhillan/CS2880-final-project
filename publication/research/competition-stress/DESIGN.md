# Fresh-seed competition stress test
Frozen before the first result on these seeds, November 13, 2026.

Analyze 100 fresh blocks (seeds 2026111300 through 2026111399), with 64 renters and 64 listings, using the four supported June–August 2026 NYC boroughs in the team's Stage 1 calibration design. Use ordinary and affordability-enriched sampling independently, and both five-listing and ceil(40%-of-options) quotas. For each market keep its people, apartments, landlord priorities, latent normal draws and base uniform random shortlist orders fixed over the nine price/noise pairs {1,2,4} x {0.5,0.75,1.25}. Freeze the uniform order at the team's selected base market ID (4,0.5), since changing the ID in the team's exposure seed otherwise re-randomizes unrelated control shortlists. Use the team's transparent score at each price setting. All other market parameters use the feature/protocol defaults at 981ed97.

Primary outcome: percentage-point change in lower-income provisional matching from uniform lists for both groups to uniform lists for lower-income renters and transparent lists for higher-income renters. Show all four design/budget panels at the calibrated point and at every other point. Use the team estimator which pools numerators and denominators across blocks within borough-month, then standardizes with the prior common recent-mover borough weights renormalized across the four boroughs and equal month weights. Report pointwise percentile intervals and a simultaneous band over all 36 effects from 10,000 paired block-bootstrap draws (seed 130155). The simultaneous critical value is the 95th percentile of the maximum absolute centered bootstrap deviation standardized by each contrast's bootstrap standard deviation. These intervals describe simulation uncertainty conditional on the data and behavioral model. Secondary: higher-income match rates, lower-group losses and gains by whether every positive-degree feasible listing remained visible, and both-groups-transparent levels. Compare GPU DA to the repository's CPU reference for every arm/grid/market in the first seed, and check stability/feasibility of every allocation. No changes to policy arms after observing outcomes.
