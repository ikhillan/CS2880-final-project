# Can a platform offer one more attainable option without displacing people?

This is an internal, time-stamped analysis plan, written after the earlier PAES results and before solving any of the residual-seat interventions below. The 2026 cohort was previously examined under other policies. Every result is a fixed-report, fixed-score counterfactual.

## Two-stage policy

Take the original five-choice display and its provisional applicant-proposing DA allocation. Compute remaining slots at each program and find the previously unmatched applicants with at least one submitted eligible program with remaining slots. Among these applicants and seats, compute a maximum-cardinality feasible assignment. Each selected applicant gets one previously hidden program from this assignment in place of their lowest-ranked baseline displayed program, independently of the other applicants' decisions. Everybody else keeps their menu. Run applicant-proposing DA using all priorities and the revised menus. The assignment witness keeps all provisional offers and adds the proposed pairs. Applicants who opt out can retain their original lists and the unused referrals vanish. The comparison presumes that the institution observes tentative offers, can limit disclosure to the selected programs, and both stages have the same capacity and preferences. It also presumes that the platform has candidate preferences or a vetted pool to identify acceptable alternatives even when those options were not on a first-stage shortlist.

2026 primary starting display: the previously fitted rank-informed neural offer predictor, replaying the stored three H200 fits' ensemble and exact tie handling. Other starting displays: the rank-blind network (2026), historical cutoff when past cohort data exist (2025/26), first five and one original fixed-seed uniform display for each 2024–26. All include the full applicant denominator. A secondary calculation tests the network result on seats excluding overbooking and includes 2024–26 first-one menus for a more binding quota. Use the published score and tie ordering for final clearance. Baselines are pinned and there is no fit to these intervention outcomes.

Comparators on every panel: (1) every eligible unmatched applicant takes their individually favorite submitted hidden option with positive residual capacity without coordinating; (2) serially reserve one available residual seat per applicant in a prespecified independent random order, each taking their favorite option available when their turn comes; (3) maximum residual b-matching with no option weighting, by a flow solver and fixed seeded student and program permutations, never using identities as a welfare objective. Each comparator edits only one slot. Maximum flow optimizes the number of referrals; the identity of those selected by any one maximum flow need not be unique. Prespecified lottery seed 2880781. On 2026 informed only, repeat the central matching with 16 additional random student and program permutations to measure selection sensitivity and distinct recipient identities; record aggregate data only.

Quantify the preliminary match count, unmatched, unfilled, unmatched with no submitted/intersection edge to an idle school, unmatched with any such edge, Hall deficiency among that set, and maximum achievable extra count preserving every original contract. Check an integral flow witness and equally sized cut. Count higher/lower scored program rank (where applicable), actual losses/gains of previous placements, weak and strict applicant-rank improvements, retained baseline edges and who actually enrolls under counterfactual DA (placements only). Independent batch DA verifies the exact final partner array for every primary repair; direct verifier checks stability/capacity at witness and realized allocations. The certified method on 2026 informed also evaluates optional take-up at 25%, 50%, 75% by a fixed independent uniform lottery. Report how many beneficiaries have a historical or full-report offer as well as those missed by both reference outcomes.

Forecast variant (2026 informed only) stress-tests the distinguishing information requirement. Infer tentative allocation using public reported score priorities with an independent tie lottery, then with seeded additive N(0,10^2) and N(0,30^2) score error; compute coordinated residual assignments and clear the resulting menu using actual benchmark priorities. Predicted and realized baseline positions need not coincide. No no-harm guarantee applies when the platform commits changes before observing its actual tentative allocation. Publish actual gains/losses under all three. No protected-status fairness claim.
