# Rank quality of a vacancy referral

The first maximum residual matching for the 2026 informed network assigned the median new recipient the fourteenth program they had submitted. This matching had arbitrary score-independent tie-breaking. On the same primary cohort, hold baseline offers fixed. Compute the maximum number of entrants for each possible ordinal rank ceiling 1 through 20. At each ceiling minimize the sum of submitted ranks among extensions of greatest cardinality. Use a capacitated min-cost flow implemented via a sparse bipartite linear program on individual unit edges (an integral polytope), verify integrality and no capacity conflicts, and verify cardinality with independent max flow and a min cut. Real acceptance probabilities by rank are unknown. A ceiling on acceptable referral rank is a policy scenario, and minimum sum of ranks is an ordinal convention rather than interpersonal cardinal welfare.
