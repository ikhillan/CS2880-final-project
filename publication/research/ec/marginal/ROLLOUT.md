# Rollout diagnostic

Exploratory supplement specified after inspecting isolated and joint effects. Reuse the 100 summer blocks and calibrated ranks. Use 24 fresh seeded random permutations of higher-income applicants per market. For each permutation compute the complete prefix policy path, from all-uniform through every higher applicant switched, for each of the two score price coefficients. Conditional on the number switched under independent adoption probability p, the switched set is a uniformly sampled subset of that size. Average the prefix outcomes and mix them with exact binomial weights at p in {0,.01,.025,.05,.1,.2,.35,.5,.75,1}. Retain the complete grid including signs near zero. Use a paired whole-block bootstrap over the original 100 blocks; it reflects the market and finite permutation draws together. The endpoint and initial derivative have separately verified exact values for the fixed sample. These computations are not independent evidence of take-up by people.
