# Winter catalogue transfer of the score-only intervention

Fixed on November 13, 2026 before simulation of this comparison on the January–March catalogues. These catalogues were used previously for a cheapest-first intervention in a different generator. This replication concerns a different score, catalogue cleaning and preference model. It reuses the calibrated summer model and donor pool, so it cannot establish transfer to new households or observed behavior.

Use January, February and March 2026 FirstMover records. Apply the calibrated generator's normal within-month data cleaning. Exclude ambiguous physical identities and any winter record whose listing ID or normalized (borough, street, unit) was seen in the uncleaned summer June–August 2026 records. Remove winter cross-month repeats by those keys. Use only calibration renter donors. Freeze all feature standardization, quality references, PUMA locations and neighborhood labels to the summer development catalogue. January–March nominal rents are converted using the recorded national monthly CPI-U values in July dollars. No September–October 2026 or summer 2025 market outcomes enter this test.

Use seeds 2026111700 through 2026111779, with one 64-renter/64-listing market per supported borough-month (Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens) per seed, for ordinary and affordability-enriched sampling. Record every failure. U/U is uniform for all. U/T1 and U/T4 change only the higher-income shortlists to the transparent score with price coefficients 1 and 4, respectively. Keep every lower-income shortlist, every participant, both sides' priorities and all random shocks identical inside each market. Renter utility is fixed at the calibrated price penalty 4 and private taste noise 0.5. Give each individual the same number of recommendations in each of the three policies. Use top-five and ceil(40% of feasible degree) with full-list/open handling matching the original generator.

The primary endpoint is low-income match-rate effect of changing the higher-income scoring weight from 4 to 1 in ordinary top-five markets, equivalently (U/T1 − U/U) − (U/T4 − U/U). Report all three levels, both spillovers, and their paired contrast. Secondary panels use ordinary proportional, enriched top-five, and enriched proportional. For all endpoints pool numerator and denominator separately in each borough-month before applying the fixed ACS recent-mover shares and equal month weights conditional on the four boroughs. Report any missing cell/seed, observed weight and the denominators. If a design loses a full cell, report its supported-cell conditional effect and explicitly report the lost portion. Never change the ordinary target to accommodate the enriched design. Use 10,000 whole-seed paired bootstrap draws (seed 871136), pointwise CIs for the single primary endpoint, and one simultaneous family over the three contrasts in all estimable panels for secondary interpretation. The resulting uncertainty is Monte Carlo uncertainty conditional on inputs and model. Preserve all outcomes regardless of sign.
